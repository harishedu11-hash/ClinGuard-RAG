GASTROESOPHAGEAL REFLUX DISEASE (GERD)

Symptoms:
- Heartburn
- Regurgitation
- Symptoms worse after meals
- Symptoms worse when lying down

Management:
- Weight loss
- Avoid late meals
- Lifestyle modification

Treatment:
- Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs)
- H2 receptor blockers


COMMUNITY ACQUIRED PNEUMONIA

Symptoms:
- Fever
- Chills
- Productive cough
- Lung consolidation

Common Bacterial Cause:
- Streptococcus pneumoniae

Management:
- Clinical evaluation
- Chest imaging when appropriate

Treatment:
- Appropriate antibiotic therapy


HYPERTENSION

Definition:
- Persistently elevated blood pressure

Management:
- Lifestyle modification
- Blood pressure monitoring

Treatment:
- ACE inhibitors
- ARBs
- Calcium channel blockers
- Thiazide diuretics


DRUG INTERACTION

ACE Inhibitors:
- Lisinopril

Potential Risk:
- Hyperkalemia

Potential interacting medications:
- Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX)

Clinical concern:
- Increased risk of severe hyperkalemia
