### DOCUMENT

TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS

Findings:
- Polyuria
- Polydipsia
- Polyphagia
- Fatigue

Management:
- Lifestyle modification
- Metformin
- Glycemic control

Associated Conditions:
- Chronic Kidney Disease
- Coronary Artery Disease
- Peripheral Neuropathy

Clinical Concern:
- Increased infection risk

CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE

Findings:
- Fatigue
- Edema
- Hypertension

Management:
- Blood pressure control
- Renal function monitoring

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- Cardiovascular disease

Clinical Concern:
- Drug dose adjustment


### DOCUMENT

HEART FAILURE

Findings:
- Dyspnea
- Orthopnea
- Peripheral edema

Management:
- ACE inhibitors
- Beta blockers
- Diuretics

Associated Conditions:
- Hypertension
- Coronary Artery Disease
- CKD

Clinical Concern:
- Avoid NSAIDs when possible


### DOCUMENT

HYPERTENSION

Findings:
- Elevated blood pressure
- Headache
- Often asymptomatic

Management:
- Blood pressure monitoring
- Lifestyle modification
- Antihypertensive therapy

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- Chronic Kidney Disease
- Heart Failure

Clinical Concern:
- Cardiovascular risk


### DOCUMENT

ACUTE KIDNEY INJURY

Findings:
- Rising creatinine
- Reduced urine output
- Fluid overload

Management:
- Identify underlying cause
- Review medications
- Monitor renal function

Associated Conditions:
- CKD
- Sepsis
- Nephrotoxic medications

Clinical Concern:
- Rapid deterioration of kidney function
