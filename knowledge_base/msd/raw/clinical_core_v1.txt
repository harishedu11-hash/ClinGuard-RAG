========================================================
CARDIOMETABOLIC DOMAIN
========================================================

TYPE 2 DIABETES MELLITUS

Symptoms:
- Polyuria
- Polydipsia
- Polyphagia
- Fatigue
- Weight loss

Management:
- Lifestyle modification
- Weight reduction
- Glycemic control

Treatment:
- Metformin
- SGLT2 inhibitors
- GLP-1 receptor agonists
- Insulin

Associated Conditions:
- Hypertension
- Chronic Kidney Disease
- Coronary Artery Disease
- Peripheral Neuropathy
- Retinopathy
- Foot Ulcers
- Recurrent Infections

Contraindications:
- Severe renal dysfunction may require treatment adjustment

--------------------------------------------------------

HYPERTENSION

Symptoms:
- Often asymptomatic
- Headache
- Dizziness

Management:
- Blood pressure monitoring
- Salt restriction
- Weight loss

Treatment:
- ACE inhibitors
- ARBs
- Calcium channel blockers
- Thiazide diuretics

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- CKD
- CAD
- Heart Failure

Contraindications:
- Monitor renal function in CKD

--------------------------------------------------------

HEART FAILURE

Symptoms:
- Dyspnea
- Orthopnea
- Peripheral edema
- Fatigue

Management:
- Volume assessment
- Daily weight monitoring

Treatment:
- ACE inhibitors
- Beta blockers
- Diuretics
- SGLT2 inhibitors

Associated Conditions:
- Hypertension
- CAD
- CKD
- Atrial Fibrillation

Contraindications:
- NSAIDs may worsen heart failure

--------------------------------------------------------

CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE

Symptoms:
- Fatigue
- Edema
- Hypertension

Management:
- Renal function monitoring
- Control blood pressure

Treatment:
- ACE inhibitors
- ARBs when appropriate

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- Hypertension
- Cardiovascular Disease

Contraindications:
- Avoid nephrotoxic medications
- Avoid unnecessary NSAIDs

========================================================
RESPIRATORY DOMAIN
========================================================

COMMUNITY ACQUIRED PNEUMONIA

Symptoms:
- Fever
- Chills
- Productive cough
- Dyspnea
- Lung consolidation

Common Organisms:
- Streptococcus pneumoniae
- Haemophilus influenzae

Management:
- Clinical assessment
- Chest imaging when indicated

Treatment:
- Appropriate antibiotic therapy

Associated Conditions:
- COPD
- Diabetes
- Immunocompromised states

--------------------------------------------------------

COPD

Symptoms:
- Chronic cough
- Sputum production
- Dyspnea

Management:
- Smoking cessation
- Pulmonary rehabilitation

Treatment:
- Bronchodilators
- Inhaled therapies

Associated Conditions:
- Smoking history
- Cardiovascular disease

========================================================

TRAUMA AND EMERGENCY DOMAIN
========================================================

HEMORRHAGIC SHOCK

Findings:
- Hypotension
- Tachycardia
- Altered mental status

Management:
- Rapid bleeding control
- Fluid resuscitation
- Blood transfusion

--------------------------------------------------------

ABDOMINAL TRAUMA

Findings:
- Abdominal tenderness
- Distension
- Hypotension

Management:
- FAST examination
- Surgical evaluation if unstable

--------------------------------------------------------

TENSION PNEUMOTHORAX

Findings:
- Respiratory distress
- Hypotension
- Decreased breath sounds

Management:
- Immediate decompression
- Chest tube placement

========================================================

DRUG INTERACTION DOMAIN
========================================================

LISINOPRIL + TMP-SMX

Risk:
- Severe hyperkalemia

Recommendation:
- Monitor potassium closely
- Consider alternative therapy

--------------------------------------------------------

WARFARIN + ANTIBIOTICS

Risk:
- Elevated INR
- Increased bleeding risk

Recommendation:
- Monitor INR carefully

--------------------------------------------------------

NSAIDS + CKD

Risk:
- Acute kidney injury
- Worsening renal function

Recommendation:
- Avoid when possible
