COMMUNITY ACQUIRED PNEUMONIA

Findings:
- Fever
- Chills
- Productive cough
- Dyspnea
- Lung consolidation

Common Organisms:
- Streptococcus pneumoniae
- Haemophilus influenzae
- Mycoplasma pneumoniae

Management:
- Clinical evaluation
- Chest imaging
- Appropriate antibiotic therapy

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- COPD
- Immunocompromised state
