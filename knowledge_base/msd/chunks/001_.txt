========================================================
RESPIRATORY DOMAIN
========================================================

COMMUNITY ACQUIRED PNEUMONIA

Findings:
- Fever
- Chills
- Productive cough
- Dyspnea
- Lung consolidation

Common Organisms:
- Streptococcus pneumoniae
- Haemophilus influenzae
- Mycoplasma pneumoniae

Management:
- Clinical evaluation
- Chest imaging
- Antibiotic therapy when indicated

Associated Conditions:
- Diabetes
- COPD
- Immunocompromised state

--------------------------------------------------------

COPD

Findings:
- Chronic cough
- Sputum production
- Dyspnea

Management:
- Smoking cessation
- Bronchodilators

========================================================
DRUG INTERACTION
========================================================

LISINOPRIL + TMP-SMX

Risk:
- Severe hyperkalemia

Mechanism:
- Potassium retention

Management:
- Monitor potassium
- Consider alternative antibiotic

--------------------------------------------------------

WARFARIN + ANTIBIOTICS

Risk:
- Elevated INR
- Bleeding

Management:
- Monitor INR
- Consider dose adjustment

--------------------------------------------------------

NSAIDS + CKD

Risk:
- Acute kidney injury
- Worsening renal function

Management:
- Avoid if possible
- Monitor renal function

--------------------------------------------------------

NSAIDS + HEART FAILURE

Risk:
- Worsening heart failure
- Fluid retention

Management:
- Avoid when possible

========================================================

TRAUMA
========================================================

HEMORRHAGIC SHOCK

Findings:
- Hypotension
- Tachycardia
- Altered mental status

Management:
- Bleeding control
- Blood transfusion
- Fluid resuscitation

--------------------------------------------------------

ABDOMINAL TRAUMA

Findings:
- Distended abdomen
- Tenderness
- Hypotension

Management:
- FAST exam
- Surgical evaluation

--------------------------------------------------------

OPEN FRACTURE

Findings:
- Bone exposed
- Severe pain

Management:
- Early antibiotics
- Orthopedic consultation

========================================================

INFECTIOUS DISEASE
========================================================

PYELONEPHRITIS

Findings:
- Fever
- Flank pain
- Costovertebral angle tenderness
- Pyuria

Management:
- Antibiotics
- Consider admission if severe

Associated Conditions:
- Pregnancy
- Diabetes

--------------------------------------------------------

SEPSIS

Findings:
- Fever
- Tachycardia
- Hypotension

Management:
- Blood cultures
- Broad spectrum antibiotics
- Fluid resuscitation
