OPEN FRACTURE

Findings:
- Bone exposed
- Severe pain

Management:
- Early antibiotics
- Orthopedic consultation

INFECTIOUS DISEASE

PYELONEPHRITIS

Findings:
- Fever
- Flank pain
- Costovertebral angle tenderness
- Pyuria

Management:
- Antibiotics
- Consider admission if severe

Associated Conditions:
- Pregnancy
- Diabetes
